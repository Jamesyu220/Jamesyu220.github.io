Projects
Physics-based Simulation of Gas Particles
James Yu, Ping-Chuan Lin, Chun-Fu Kuo, Felicity Nielson, Andy Yu, Ziming Liu

A real-time simulation of over 1000 ideal gas particles within a confined space. The measured pressure achieved 95% in accuracy verified by Ideal Gas Law.

Virtual Fluid Manipulation via Hand Motions
Barnabas Borcsok, Justin G. Wit, James Yu, Joseph P. Harding

A real-time fluid simulator incorporated hand motion detector, the users can control the virtual fluid by their hands. The hand motion detector was optimized and tailored for the fluid simulation, so the inference time was 12 times faster than MediaPipe.

Detecting Disaster Tweets by Machine Learning Methods
Rodrigo B. Loza, Justin G. Wit, James Yu, Paul M. Cucchiara, Mohit Gupta

A Machine Learning project utilized vectorizing, PCA dimension reduction, K-Means algorithm, Gaussian Mixture Model, Support Vector Machine, Naive Bayes to detect Twitter posts (Tweets) related to disastrous events. This model achieved over 80% in both precision and recall when testing on a dataset of 1000 Tweets.
